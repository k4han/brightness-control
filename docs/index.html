Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about compatibility, setup, and features.

Why is my external monitor not responding to brightness changes?
1. Enable DDC/CI in OSD: Open your monitor's On-Screen Display (OSD) menu using its physical buttons, locate the DDC/CI setting, and ensure it is turned On.
2. Check Video Cable: Connect your monitor directly via HDMI or DisplayPort. Some low-quality USB-C hubs, docking stations, or KVM switches filter out DDC/CI I2C signals.
Can the app automatically launch when Windows boots?
Yes! You can enable "Start with Windows" in the Settings panel. When enabled, the application starts quietly minimized in your system tray without interrupting your startup routine.
What keyboard shortcuts and presets are supported?
- F5: Rescan and refresh connected displays.
- Esc: Minimize immediately to the system tray.
- Quick preset buttons (25%, 50%, 75%, 100%) and ±5% stepper buttons on each display card.
Is Brightness Control safe and free to use?
Yes, Brightness Control is 100% free and open-source under the permissive MIT License. There are zero ads, telemetry trackers, or external data collection. The entire codebase is transparent and inspectable on GitHub.
